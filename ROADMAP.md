# Study Roadmap

The module numbers provide a stable reference. The sequence below groups related material so that each phase builds on the previous one.

| Phase | Study order | Exit project |

## Pace options

### Focused · 12 weeks

- Weeks 1–2: modules 01–04
- Weeks 3–5: modules 05–10
- Weeks 6–7: modules 11–13 and 19
- Weeks 8–9: modules 14–18
- Weeks 10–12: modules 20–21 and mock interviews

Expect 10–12 focused hours per week.

### Sustainable · 24 weeks

- Weeks 1–4: modules 01–04
- Weeks 5–10: modules 05–10
- Weeks 11–14: modules 11–13 and 19
- Weeks 15–19: modules 14–18
- Weeks 20–24: modules 20–21 and mock interviews

Expect 5–7 focused hours per week.

## Weekly study loop

1. **Learn** — use the module's বাংলা deep-learning prompt for one topic at a time, then write short notes in your own words.
2. **Compare** — make a trade-off table for competing approaches.
3. **Apply** — complete the module exercise using the design template.
4. **Defend** — explain the design aloud in 10–15 minutes.
5. **Review** — answer the checkpoint questions without notes.
6. **Record** — update [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md) and schedule a review.

## Review cadence

Revisit completed modules after 1 day, 1 week, 1 month, and before an interview. During review, focus on decisions and failure modes rather than definitions.

## Interview practice ladder

| Level | Time | Goal |
|---|---:|---|
| Guided | 60–90 min | Use notes and the full template |
| Independent | 45–60 min | Use the template without topic notes |
| Timed | 35–45 min | Simulate a real interview |
| Adversarial | 45 min | Ask a partner to change scale and requirements |

Move forward when two consecutive attempts cover requirements, estimates, APIs/data, architecture, bottlenecks, reliability, and trade-offs.
